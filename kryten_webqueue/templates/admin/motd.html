Weekend Poster Grid

Titles come from the weekend schedule workbook. Each is resolved to an IMDb link and poster art automatically; slots with no title yet, or a title that can't be verified, show a mystery box. Override any slot below to pin a title, upload alternate art, or point the link somewhere other than IMDb. Overrides apply to the selected weekend only and retire on their own once that week rolls over. Publishing renders the currently scheduled template and replaces the whole channel MOTD (the previous one is backed up).
